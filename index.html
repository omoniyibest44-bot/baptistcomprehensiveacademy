ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

About Baptist Comprehensive Academy

A Christ-centred learning community focused on knowledge, character and service.

Who We Are

Baptist Comprehensive Academy is a school designed to support academic development alongside strong moral and Christian values. Located at Ogo Oluwa Baptist Church premises in Masaka.

Our goal is to create an environment where students can learn, grow, discover their abilities and prepare for responsible service in their communities.

Our Core Pillars

  • Knowledge: We value learning, critical thinking, and continuous academic improvement.
  • Character: We encourage integrity, self-discipline, respect, and honesty in all dealings.
  • Service: We teach students to use their talents to help others and transform society.
  • Faith: We promote Christian principles in daily school life and spiritual growth.
